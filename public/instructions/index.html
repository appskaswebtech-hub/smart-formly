Features

What Smartformly can do

Powerful capabilities that go beyond a basic form builder.

Conditional logic

Show or hide fields based on previous answers. Guide respondents through only the questions that are relevant to them.

Multi-step forms

Split long forms into bite-sized pages with a progress bar. Reduces drop-off on complex surveys.

Email notifications

Response analytics

View submission counts, completion rates, and per-question breakdowns — all in a live dashboard.

Scheduling Pro

Embedding

Add your form to any website

Choose how the form appears on your page.

Inline embed

Place the form directly into your page content using a standard <iframe>. Resizes to fit its container.

Slide-in popup

The form slides in from the corner on a trigger — scroll depth, time on page, or a button click.

Tips

Get better responses

Small tweaks that make a big difference to completion rates.

Keep it short Forms with fewer than 5 fields get 3× more completions. Only ask what you actually need.
Use "Required" sparingly Only mark a field as required if a blank answer would make the response useless.
Add a progress bar Showing respondents how far along they are reduces abandonment on multi-step forms.
Avoid jargon Write field labels and placeholder text as if explaining to someone unfamiliar with your business.
FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers to things people ask most often.

How do I export my responses?
Go to your form's Responses tab and click Export in the top-right corner. You can download a .csv file or push data directly to Google Sheets if you have the integration connected.
Can respondents save a form and come back later?
Yes — enable Save & Resume under Form Settings → Behavior. Respondents will receive a unique link by email to return to their in-progress submission.
How does conditional logic work?
Select any field and click Logic in the field panel. Set a rule like "Show this field if [Field X] equals [Value Y]." Multiple conditions can be combined with AND/OR. Conditional logic is available on all paid plans.
Is there a limit on how many responses I can receive?
The Free plan allows up to 100 responses per month per form. Pro and Team plans have no response limits. You can also set a manual cap under Form Settings → Scheduling to close a form after a specific count.
How do I prevent the same person from submitting twice?
Go to Form Settings → Behavior and enable Limit one response per email. For logged-in users, you can also enable Limit one response per account.
Can I accept payments through a form?
Yes. Add a Payment field and connect your Stripe account under Settings → Integrations. Payments are processed securely through Stripe — Smartformly never stores card data. Payment fields are available on the Pro plan and above.
